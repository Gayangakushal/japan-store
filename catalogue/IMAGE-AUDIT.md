# Ramen image audit

Checked 2026-11-01. No production migration or deployment performed.

**27 verified, locally optimized photos; 23 unique branded placeholders.**

Every photo below was visually checked for brand, flavor, and whether it depicts a single packet/cup. Manufacturer and image search results were used for matching, not as a permission grant. Downloaded photos come from Open Food Facts under CC BY-SA 3.0 or Wikimedia Commons under the per-file licence; attribution and licence links are displayed in the storefront and the public credits page. Placeholder entries have no claim of depicting the actual packet.

The storefront now reads all 50 entries directly from ramen.json. Catalogue-only IDs are namespaced strings; existing commercial rows are matched by catalogue ID, slug or normalized name to prevent duplicate cards. Existing numeric IDs and LKR prices are preserved from the checked-in snapshot or already-cached store data.

- Nissin Raoh candidate 0070662500033 showed Tonkotsu, not Miso.
- Samyang candidate 8801073114333 showed Corn, not Cream Carbonara.
- Acecook Pho Bo candidate 8934561020035 was Vifon, the wrong manufacturer.

- MyKuali White Curry candidate 9555655005203 showed a 4-pack box, not the selected single retail packet.
- Koka candidate 8888056815227 showed 4 x 90g; the separate single-cup photo was selected instead.
- Habanero Lime candidate 8801073143777 belonged to a 675g multi-pack record; a verified single 135g packet photo was used instead.

## Local frontend mode

No database connection, migration change, migration application, or deployment was performed for the local frontend task. All 50 products are rendered from JSON; no database rollout is needed to display them. Future deployment requires approval.
